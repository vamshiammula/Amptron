# Reference decisions

Reviewed September 2026. Borrow interaction principles, not branding or competitor claims.

- [OpenAI Design Guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/): clear distinction between wordmark, symbol, spacing, typography and usage rules. Apply that consistency to the Amptron system.
- [Ather](https://www.atherenergy.com/): direct paths to test rides, model comparisons, finance and dealers. Amptron should expose clear rider decisions and show price context rather than an unexplained monthly figure.
- [NIU](https://global.niu.com/en-us): vehicle families and ownership/support navigation. Amptron's planned family needs visible status, not fictitious live product categories.
- [GOV.UK form structure](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/form-structure): structure forms around the information needed for the user's task. Preserve accessible labels and useful error recovery in the existing shared form.

Chosen translation: product-led hero, restrained graphite/paper surfaces, electric green for action, transparent request language, and a dedicated rider-support entry point. Keep admin work focused on data and actions rather than marketing decoration.
